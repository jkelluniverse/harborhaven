## Home Watch, in detail
Florida is hard on empty houses. AC fails, humidity climbs, pests move in, a slow leak becomes a floor. Home Watch is a scheduled visual inspection that catches these early.

- Weekly or bi-weekly visits, on a schedule you choose
- Photo report after every visit, time-stamped
- Same-day call if we find a problem, and we coordinate the fix with your vendor or ours
- Key holder service for contractors, deliveries, and emergencies
- Storm prep and post-storm checks
- Pre-arrival prep and post-departure lock-up

Fully insured. Set up remotely if you're not in town.

## Permits & Projects, in detail
**Permit only.** Licensed contractors and homeowners who need a City of Sarasota permit but can't pull one themselves: we pull it under our general contractor's license. Flat fee, fast turnaround.

**Managed projects.** From driveway and decking to painting, fencing, tile, roofing, stucco, gutters, concrete, electrical, and plumbing — we quote it, permit it if required, hire from our vetted subcontractor list, track every dollar, and walk it with you at the end before the final bill.

You get one estimate, one point of contact, staged billing on larger jobs, and a link to pay by bank transfer or card.
